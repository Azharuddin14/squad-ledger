Database

What do these buttons do?

Download DB exports every player, team, and saved match as one JSON file. Restoring replaces everything currently loaded. Download Players Only exports just the player list (ratings/skills) — a much smaller file, meant for importing ratings elsewhere (e.g. Squad Ledger) without also handing over the full match archive. Import Squads loads one era's squads from a Squad Ledger export: players are matched by name and updated in place, so matches, records, career stats and seasons are kept.
